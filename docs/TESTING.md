Every line counts down once a second; leave it open for a minute and the minutes tick over.
2. A shower. `/shower in 40`: the first line reads "Meteor Shower in 40s" within a second and counts down; at zero it turns gold, "Meteor Shower! 2:59 left", and counts down in m:ss (the HUD banner "Meteor Shower!" shows too). `/shower end`: the line goes back to white, "Meteor Shower in 1h 59m" (or less, by the clock).
3. A round. `/rush in 40`: the second line reads "Catch Rush in 40s" and counts; at zero it turns gold, "Catch Rush now! 1:30 left". `/rush end` settles the round and the line returns to white and a time to the next one. A forced `/rush` (no argument) shows "Catch Rush now! 1:30 left" at once.
4. The Peddler. There is no Peddler dev command: the ship lands 20 s after the server boots and then every 5 minutes (`Config.PeddlerIntervalSeconds`, measured from one landing to the next). Open the Quests screen before the 20 s are up: the third line counts down "Peddler lands in 19s"; when the banner "The Peddler has landed!" shows the line turns gold, "Peddler leaves in 1:30", and counts to 0:01; after the ship lifts it reads white again, "Peddler lands in 3m" (a landing 5 minutes after the last one). Join a second client (Test > Clients and Servers) in the middle of a visit: its card shows the gold "Peddler leaves in ..." at once, and its Trade button works (a late joiner's visit used to reach only the ship).
5. The season. `/season HauntedNebula`: the fourth line becomes "Haunted Nebula: 16d left" (a forced season counts to the real end, 2026-10-30 16:00 UTC, so the days from today; after the real end it shows the whole window, 14d) and the Event tab joins; the weekly line is gone while the season runs. `/season off`: "Alien of the Week in ..." returns.
6. Closed means still. With the screen open watch `PlayerGui.QuestsScreen.Panel.WhatsOn.Line1` in Explorer change its Text each second. Close the screen, wait ten seconds, open it: the text jumped by about ten seconds (it was not redrawn while closed) and is right at once.
7. iPhone SE. In the device emulator at iPhone SE (667x375): the card is one band of two lines in two columns under the title line, clear of the Close button; no line ends in an ellipsis, at rest and with `/shower` and `/rush` running at the same time ("Meteor Shower! 2:59 left" beside "Catch Rush now! 1:30 left"); Field Notes still shows all five steps, the current step's card whole (title, legend, objectives, rewards, Claim); Daily & Weekly shows the heading and two quests (the third is a scroll away); the Event tab shows the title, the hint and two rows. Repeat at iPad (1080x810) and a portrait phone: the lines scale up, none is cut.
8. The home place (`home.project.json`). Open the Quests screen: the Meteor Shower line and the weekly line show; the Rush and the Peddler lines are blank (no rounds or visits at home).

Known gaps in this milestone: no icons on the lines (no room beside the sentences at phone size, and only the Shower and Peddler icons exist); the weekly drop yields its line to a running season (the HUD banner does the same); the season line reads "Haunted Nebula: 16d left" because "ends in" is too long for a long name; a Dev-forced shower is not foreseen by the Catch Rush's count (the next round after it may then be passed over by the live clock only).
