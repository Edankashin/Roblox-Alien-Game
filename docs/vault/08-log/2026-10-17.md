# 2026-10-17

## Research worker: blocky Roblox pet style

- Done (for [[Decisions]] B43): notes on the blocky pet look in `research_notes/Blocky Roblox pet design/blocky_visual_language.md` (seven questions: games, sub-styles, grammar, premium versus cheap, size, artist statements, the owner's five references).
- Found: five sub-styles (bevelled-cube simulator pet with a texture face; literal cube with a decal face, as in Bee Swarm; part-built; voxel cube-cell; pixel-texture box models). The bevelled cube is the genre default. The owner's references sit at the voxel and pixel-face end. BIG Games' 2023 DMCA wave treated face, texture and model likeness in the cube style as actionable, so an original roster must differ in face design and silhouette, not only in colour.
- Numbers: the 2020 DevForum tutorial bevels 0.2 on a 2-unit cube (about 10%) with 6 segments (3 is enough); pet faces are 512 px textures; PS99 community mesh scales normal 0.75, Huge 2, Titanic 6, and BIG Games' Gargantuan is 3x Titanic; avatar about 5 studs.
- Limits: devforum.roblox.com and fandom.com were refused by the egress proxy and WebFetch resolved no host, so the web findings are search snippets; no PS99, Bee Swarm, Grow a Garden or Brainrot image was viewed.
- Left: check in-game screenshots of PS99, Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot and Steal an Egg before locking the style note (Board task "Blocky style note").

## Research worker: rarity ladder and VFX in top pet games

- Done: `research_notes/Blocky Roblox pet design/rarity_ladder_vfx.md`, six questions: size, detail, VFX, presence, fierce or majestic, and mobile limits. Checked against [[Roster-Redesign]] sections 1, 4 and 6.
