## Research worker: blocky pet build pipeline

- Done: notes on the blocky-pet build pipeline (modelling, textures, budgets, animation, VFX, tutorials) in `research_notes/Blocky Roblox pet design/build_pipeline.md`. Read the Roblox creator-docs repository (at 58f787e) and the Blockbench source; measured triangle counts with bpy 5.0.1. DevForum, note.com and blockbench.net are blocked, so those findings are search summaries.
- Found, textures: `SurfaceAppearance.ResampleMode = Pixelated` (nearest-neighbour) entered the docs on 2026-05-05. `MeshPart.TextureID` has no such option and stays bilinear (blurry pixel faces). `AlphaMode.TintMask` tints only the opaque area, which suits rarity recolours. This belongs in [[SurfaceAppearance-Capability]] once the Mac confirms it in Studio.
- Found, engine: a skinned MeshPart moving inside a Model with no Humanoid forces FastCluster rebuilds, and Roblox's fix is a Humanoid in the Model. Identical meshes share one draw call only when the SurfaceAppearance or TextureContent also match. Flipbooks switch off on low-memory phones. The Custom flipbook layout is in the docs. RenderFidelity Automatic steps down at 250 and 500 studs.
- Found, modelling (measured): a bevelled cube is 44, 108 or 188 triangles at 1, 2 or 3 segments; a 9-box pet is 396, 972 or 1,692. Blockbench 5.2 has a "Beveled Cuboid" primitive (edge 2 of 16 units) and no Bevel tool. Remesh Blocks at depth 4 plus a planar dissolve turns a sphere into 886 triangles.
- Left: the Mac probes whether Pixelated holds on a pet face in Studio and on a phone; a browser session reads DevForum 517174, 2882620, 1794034 and 3035597 for creators' exact bevel and frame numbers.
