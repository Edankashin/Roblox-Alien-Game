# Playtest 1: tester 1, notes pasted 2026-11-05

The first outside playtest since the release for playtesting (B58, 2026-11-04). Ethan sat with the tester and pasted the notes on 2026-11-05; voice was not recorded at the tester's request. Device and minutes played were not recorded: ask next time (the brief's note format wants both). World 1 v135.

## What they said, as they said it

- Onboarding lacks direction: unclear what to do or where to go.
- "Treehouse bench" item confusing: costs 540 minutes to unlock with no explanation.
- Shrine of Gar and similar named locations are unclickable and unexplained.
- Animals too easy to capture (the tester was told it is a known issue).
- No cap on animal placement: expected limited storage that expands over time.
- No mechanic for new animals to spawn or appear organically.
- "The map is really pretty." The start felt good before the direction dropped off.
- Suggestions: limited storage with upgradeable capacity; a respawn or new-discovery mechanic; a character or NPC named Jessica; make named locations interactive with a visible description; more UI/UX and alien visual polish; more arrows, directions and steps; make the game more straightforward.
- More: the UI is too empty; make the goal obvious; put arrows pointing where to go; "the bar top left is like inside of the other icons"; for the important menus don't put them in the drop-down, put a button on the screen, for example the Aliens; everything is pretty confusing.

## The three things to fix first

1. **Direction in the first five minutes.** A bigger goal card for T1 to T3, a world arrow or pointing hand at the current target (the node, the trainee, the ship), and the station plate rewritten ("+540 Scrap/min", plus one line on what a station does). Strings and Hud numbers, then a Studio check on a fresh profile.
2. **The Scrap pill under Roblox's buttons.** Move it clear of the top-left inset; check at iPhone SE and iPad.
3. **Landmarks that answer a tap.** A legend line on the Shrine, Crash Site and Cave Entrance plates, and a tap that opens the Quests screen at the matching step.

For Ethan ([[Decide-Now]] 53 to 56): the catch tuning, Aliens as a HUD button, how loud the first five minutes get, and Jessica.

## Against the brief's seven questions

1. First five minutes: stalled after the start; the hints did not carry them. 2. The catch: too easy. 3. Tiers: not mentioned. 4. HUD on a phone: the top-left overlap; device unknown. 5. The loop: not understood (storage, respawn and stations all read as missing). 6. Money: not mentioned; whether the Shop was opened is unknown. 7. Join: no bounce reported.
