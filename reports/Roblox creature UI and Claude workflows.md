# Roblox hits win on finish, not generation

The games this team is measured against (Adopt Me, Pet Simulator 99, Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, Fisch, Steal an Egg) build their creatures, worlds and UI by hand under one strict style rule and then apply a cheap finishing coat; no top studio says it uses AI-generated meshes for its pets, and the one hit whose characters do come from AI images, Steal a Brainrot, now faces a public copyright question over them ([Yahoo/AT&T](https://currently.att.yahoo.com/att/copyright-ai-slop-inside-roblox-153853304.html)). Every "model a cute pet" tutorial grows the creature out of a single cube (extrude, bevel or subdivide, smooth, one material), rarity is a recolour or a scale of the same mesh, and community budgets converge on **1,000 to 2,500 triangles and one 512 px texture or shared palette per pet**. Worlds are a hub plus gated themed zones with a hard edge, and the steps that make a map read as finished are the last ones: a treated edge, a painted path, a graded sky and local lights. "Pro" UI is a small token system (thick outlines on everything, one rounded display font with a stroke, two-tone colour by function, standard rarity colours, a red square X) plus two painted asset classes, 9-slice plates and glossy icons, that studios buy from freelancers at roughly **1,000 to 3,000 Robux a frame**. The creators who ship Roblox games with Claude (cuhfilme, lihfolk, vos_studiostt) get their results not from generation but from a loop: show Claude a screenshot of the number-one game, correct it until it is right, and have it write the lesson into an Obsidian vault; they keep Blender for meshes and Studio for finish, and every public 2026 "Claude plus Blender for Roblox" project stops at props and animation or needs an NVIDIA GPU. Claude Design's 3D mode lives only in a standalone app that **closes on 14 December 2026** with no project migration; the in-chat Design canvas is an artboard tool that exports PNG and PDF, so it belongs in this project as the review surface for UI mockups, species sheets, thumbnail comps and a hatch-timing prototype, never as a source of shipped meshes or UI. The team's pipeline already matches the best practice found; what it lacks is enforcement (hooks instead of advisory rules), four UI input and accessibility rules, the reveal's three missing beats, and three map-finishing steps. One caveat governs everything below: the research sandbox could read Roblox's Creator Docs, GitHub and Anthropic's own pages in full, but YouTube, TikTok, X, the DevForum and every wiki only as search snippets, and no top studio publishes its budgets, so claims about the top games are convergent community evidence, not studio statements.

## Every cute pet grows from one cube, and rarity is a recolour

Roblox pets come in two production styles that should never be mixed in one roster. The "simulator" style of the Pet Simulator lineage, taught by the canonical DevForum tutorial from around spring 2020, starts from a cube, loop-cuts and bevels it (**"Ctrl + B ... .2 ... 6 segments"**), duplicates beveled-cube paws and ears, extrudes a tail, and sets faces to smooth; a commenter's curved tail "caused weird shading" that more subdivisions fixed ([DevForum 517174](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creating-simulator-styled-pets-in-blender/517174)). Faces in that style are decals or image textures, not geometry ([DevForum 405839](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/block-pet-modelers/405839)). The "organic cute" style of the Adopt Me lineage uses one subdivided cage with limbs extruded from the body and spheres only for eyes and nose: Creative Bloq's four-minute cat is a cube with a Subdivision Surface modifier and mirrored sphere eyes ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/3d/heres-how-to-model-a-cute-cat-or-anything-else-in-blender-in-just-a-few-easy-steps)), Jay Grewall's course insets and extrudes arms and legs from the default cube before applying Subdivision to make a baby alien ([BlenderNation](https://bazaar.blendernation.com/?p=16979)), and a 2025 guide for Roblox says to apply the modifier at export rather than leave it live ([ARwall](https://arwall.co/blogs/arwall-blogs/how-to-model-in-blender)). What makes either style read as one creature rather than assembled shapes is the same four things: continuous geometry (extrude, not place), a bevel or subdivision applied before export, Shade Smooth over the whole body, and one material or palette texture so no join has a colour seam. The alien game's grammar (round body, big eyes, short limbs, one bold colour) is the organic style, which means its tutorial steps are one cage, extruded limbs, applied subdivision, smooth shading and one material; the simulator style tolerates a few separate parts only because they are few, chunky and obey one bevel rule, and that tolerance disappears when parts are many, thin and intersecting ([Character-Pipeline.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Character-Pipeline.md)).

Colour without seams is a solved problem. The low-poly convention is one palette texture of colour squares shared by every model "to avoid draw calls", with UV islands moved onto the squares; Imphenzia's PixPal palette is CC0 and a forum user found **32 x 32 px per colour** fine where 1 px was too low ([PixPal thread](https://forum.babylonjs.com/t/imphenzia-pixpal-palette-texture-pbr-node-material-example-updated-to-version-1-2/56066)). Vertex colour is a Roblox-endorsed alternative but only on plain MeshParts and only through FBX or glTF; an October 2025 feature request confirms it "has no effect on surface appearance rendering" ([DevForum 3981469](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/vertex-coloring-support-for-surfaceappearance/3981469); [DevForum 3050119](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3050119)). Roblox's own texture guidance is **256 px for roughly 5-stud objects, 512 for 10 and 1024 for 20** ([Roblox docs: texture specifications](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/art/modeling/texture-specifications)), DevForum guidance keeps character models "below about 2K triangles" ([DevForum 197600](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/what-is-a-good-triangle-count-for-a-mesh/197600)), and the one vendor figure is 2 to 5k triangles per PS99-style pet ([Tripo3D](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/explore/pet-simulator-99-3d-model)). The cute reading itself is Lorenz's baby schema: big head, huge eyes set low, round cheeks, tiny nose, soft limbs, and restraint on detail ("Hello Kitty doesn't even have a mouth") ([Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/lies-and-deception/202602/why-we-think-babies-are-so-cute); [Tuts+](https://design.tutsplus.com/articles/the-elements-of-cute-character-design--vector-3533)). The only studio that documents its creature rule is Uplift Games: boil each Adopt Me pet "down to its most distinguishing feature and then enhance and exaggerate it", keeping "the cup feet and big eyes" ([Brands Untapped](https://www.brandsuntapped.com/uplift-games-vicky-huxley-and-sim-furniss-on-adopt-mes-journey-into-consumer-products/)). Its job posts describe a four-role pipeline the two-person team replaces with a written sheet and a scripted body: senior 3D character artists "skilled in C4D, Blender, Maya" with Substance and "both high and low poly art styles" ([Uplift careers](https://www.uplift.games/careers/3d-character-artist)), and a technical animator building skeletal assets ([job listing](https://www.aswift.com/job/technical-animator-remote)). Fisch's models were made by its solo developer ([Roblox wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Fisching/Fisch)); BIG Games, Splitting Point and Rumble publish nothing about pet production, and no RDC talk on creature art surfaced.

Rarity never means a new mesh. The table below is the shipped version of the "finishing coat": every top game carries three or four surface variants on one base mesh, and two of them add size as a second rarity axis.

| Game | Surface variants of one mesh | Size as rarity |
|---|---|---|
| Pet Simulator 99 | Normal, Golden ("coated in gold"), Rainbow, Shiny; since update 61 (May 2025) any egg can roll one ([progameguides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/how-to-make-golden-pets-in-pet-simulator-99-roblox/); [BIG Games](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-61)) | Huge, Titanic |
| Adopt Me | Normal, Neon (glow on fixed regions per species: a Lynx's tail, head, ears and back spots), Mega Neon (same regions cycling rainbow) ([Adopt Me wiki](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Lynx)) | none |
| Grow a Garden | Shiny about 31%, Golden about 6%, Rainbow about 3% (fan-maintained) ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/guides/roblox/grow-a-garden-a-complete-pet-mutations-guide/)) | weight classes from Small to Colossal ([calculator](https://www.growagardencalculator.ca/pet-weight-calculator)) |
| Steal a Brainrot | one of about ten mutations at a time: Gold, Diamond, Rainbow (about 1%), Galaxy, Lava, Celestial ([findingdulcinea](https://findingdulcinea.com/steal-a-brainrot-traits-and-mutations/)) | none |
| Steal an Egg (sold template) | Silver, Golden, Rainbow per creature ([Patreon listing](https://www.patreon.com/kinglabbok/posts/steal-egg-roblox-170613840)) | n/a |

Adopt Me's per-species Neon regions carry a structural lesson: a glow zone must be planned into the mesh as a part or material slot, not painted on afterwards, which is exactly the melt pipeline's "kept parts" ([Character-Pipeline.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Character-Pipeline.md)). Animation in the genre is cheaper than it looks: idle bob is a sine offset on the CFrame with the pet raised by half the amplitude so it never dips into the floor ([DevForum 1164331](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-fix-my-bobbing-script/1164331)), following is a RunService loop, and hatch is an egg pinned in front of the camera with a sine shake whose angle grows over time ([DevForum 2542547](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/egg-hatching-animation/2542547)).

AI is now inside Studio but built for props, not rosters. Roblox's Cube 3D is open-weight text-to-shape whose own README admits it "can produce disconnected components" on the cat example ([Roblox/cube](https://github.com/Roblox/cube/blob/main/README.md)); CubePart (May 2026) generates one mesh per named part from a user schema, the first generator aimed at coherent assembled creatures, but its weights are **8.6 GB plus 1.3 GB**, out of reach of an 8 GB Mac ([CubePart README](https://github.com/Roblox/cube/blob/main/cubepart/README.md)). Studio Assistant mesh generation (19 March 2026) makes a textured mesh from text with a triangle cap that "defaults to 10000", suggests "100s to 1K" for small objects, and exposes `generate_mesh` through the Studio MCP server ([DevForum 4527258](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/assistant-updates-mesh-generation-new-mcp-server-tools-screenshot-tool-and-more/4527258)); a September 2026 update added mesh restyling and segmentation of imported meshes ([DevForum 4890084](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-new-texture-generation-tools-segment-any-mesh-and-image-previews/4890084)). The Texture Generator's own doc states the requirement every generator shares: a model's meshes "should be arranged in a sensible way such that a unified body is formed" ([creator-docs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/studio/texture-generator.md)). Third-party vendors admit the same limits: Meshy lists "melted/blobby details" with retopology as the fix ([Meshy](https://www.meshy.ai/features/text-to-3d)) and keeps manual retopology for "deformation-heavy characters" ([Meshy review](https://medium.com/data-science-in-your-pocket/meshy-ai-3d-generator-review-2026-the-complete-production-workflow-tested-272df212c925)); raw output "often exceeds 50,000 triangles" against the 20,000 cap ([Nilo](https://nilo.io/articles/ai-generated-roblox-assets-tutorial)); and a reviewer finds AI "works better for stylized props than for realistic characters" ([vsquad](https://vsquad.art/blog/articles/best-ai-3d-generators/)). The label "AI-made" therefore covers two different failures with different fixes. Generator output fails as melted (under-described features smoothed away, dense uneven triangles, a lighting-baked texture on throwaway UVs), fixed by specific prompts, multi-view input and a retopo-and-rebake pipeline ([visiomake](https://visiomake.com/en/blog/fix-ai-generated-3d-model-topology-blender)). Part-built creatures fail as mashed (separate primitives that intersect and keep their own silhouettes), fixed only structurally: one connected skin, limbs at least a quarter of the body width, the face placed on the finished skin, one material, and glow planned as a region. There is no toon shader on Roblox; a single `Highlight` on a grouped model is the outline route, and the inverted-hull trick is reported "very glitchy" up close ([DevForum 1851704](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/adding-a-toon-shader/1851704)). The strongest 2026 AI use in creature work is not meshes at all but Claude driving Blender for animation and iteration, as the MIT `animate-roblox-characters` skill does for R6 and R15 rigs ([GitHub](https://github.com/dillydog580/animate-roblox-characters)).

## Finished maps are hub, zones, hard edge, then light

Every top collection game separates a social hub from gated themed zones and bounds the world hard. Adopt Me's Adoption Island is "the hub for all event activities ... near the center of the map", with the Neighborhood "purely for residential purposes" ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/adopt-me-adoption-island-guide)); PS99's Spawn is "the first World 1 hub, with shop, mailbox, rewards, pet storage, index access, and leaderboards" and "areas are the main progression route" ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/areas)); Steal a Brainrot puts its conveyor "in the center of the map" with a ring of personal bases ([rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/steal-a-brainrot/updates/steal-a-brainrot-beginners-guide-2026)); Fisch's Moosewood is "the starting island, located in the center of the map" with nine more unlocking at level 10 ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/fisch-map-with-all-islands-and-npcs/)). Roblox's own environmental-art curriculum teaches the same shape from the greybox: snap to **5 studs and 90 degrees**, doorways "at least 10 studs wide" (default jump is 5 studs), pockets with "a maximum of three entrance or exit points", and placeholder colours so "if a player can see at least one of these colors, they can estimate their location" ([Greybox your environment](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment)). The construction chapter draws the unplayable ring (water or lava) before the playable area, paints a primary path, rings the border with rocks "with varied size and orientation", and surrounds everything with invisible barrier parts ([Construct your world](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world)). The team's reference videos agree: one hero landmark per zone, a pale path to it, and a world that reads as one object from the pull-out shot, "the map is built to be filmed" ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md); [Map-Dressing.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Map-Dressing.md)). Seasonal re-dressing of one fixed island is how a live game refreshes without a new layout: Adopt Me's winter build adds "snow and a frozen river" and new bridges, and its Halloween teaser showed signposts to places that did not yet exist ([findingdulcinea](https://findingdulcinea.com/adopt-me-winter-update-features/); [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/?p=214146)).

The reconstructed build order of a "Pet Sim style" map, from the curriculum and the YouTube series descriptions, is eleven steps, and the ones that separate finished from placed all come after the greybox is deleted: floors by material per zone, walls and roofs, delete the greybox and baseplate, terrain (ring first, then playable area, painted path, water), rocks and foliage, containment walls, Lighting with Atmosphere, Clouds, Bloom and DepthOfField, dust and cloud particle layers, perimeter and doorway lights, then the optimisation pass ([Construct your world](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world); [Optimize your experience](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/optimize-your-experience)). "Adopt Me style" on YouTube means pet and house systems, not a map method; the map side comes from the low-poly and simulator-map series and single-topic polish videos on borders, scenery and "stylized settings", and the DevForum consensus on the look is Blender meshes imported with "smooth plastic material, Bright colors" ([DevForum 539908](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-make-a-low-poly-build/539908)). The prop pipeline Roblox teaches is a modular kit on a 5-stud grid with pivots "at the forward-most lower corner", one square trim sheet up to 1024 px shared by kit and props, and props with "only enough geometry to read from their silhouette" ([Develop polished assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/develop-polished-assets)). The engine fact that matters more than any triangle count is instancing: meshes with the same MeshContent "share one draw call when their SurfaceAppearance (or TextureContent) and materials match", re-importing a map "can create separate asset IDs, which blocks instancing", and triangle count "matters less than draw calls" ([Improve performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve)). The repo's flat-colour, no-texture prop set with `Props.Tint` keeps instancing intact; a per-biome SurfaceAppearance swap would split draw calls ([Map-Dressing.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Map-Dressing.md)).

Lighting is mid-transition and the team's data table is behind it. `Lighting.Technology` (Voxel, ShadowMap, Future, the never-described Unified) "has been superseded by" `LightingStyle` (Realistic or Soft) plus `PrioritizeLightingQuality` ([Technology enum](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/Technology); [LightingStyle enum](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/LightingStyle)), and a 7 October 2026 tutorial already warns that older videos asking for Future or Unified are out of date ([obby.fun](https://www.obby.fun/blog/roblox-studio-realistic-lighting)). Two client-side facts shape every look: lowering a player's graphics quality "switches Realistic lighting to Soft" with no developer override ([DevForum 4027710](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/realistic-lighting-on-lower-quality-settings/4027710)), and shadows are "disabled at quality levels below 4" ([Improve performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve)), so a phone player on low settings sees voxel light and no shadows whatever the data says. No top game's lighting values are published anywhere reachable; the closest published recipe is Roblox's own sample world: **Realistic, Ambient and OutdoorAmbient 26,34,36, ShadowSoftness 0.15, GeographicLatitude -18, Atmosphere Density 0.285 with Haze 2 and Glare 0.3, Bloom 1.5 at Size 56, DepthOfField FarIntensity 0.05, Clouds Cover 0.625**, plus perimeter Neon strips carrying SurfaceLights at Range 21 and hallway SpotLights at Range 20 as orientation cues ([Construct your world](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world)). The cartoon-lighting tutorials diverge mainly by raising Brightness to 2 and dropping the sun to ClockTime 14 ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7caEtAbq4DA)). Atmosphere's own rules are worth knowing: Glare "requires Haze above 0", Decay requires both, and the effect "will not appear if you have the default skybox" ([Atmosphere](https://create.roblox.com/docs/environment/atmosphere); [DevForum 2764787](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-lighting/2764787)). The `Lighting.luau` table should therefore carry `LightingStyle` and `PrioritizeLightingQuality` instead of a Technology value, and the night and weather looks must be judged a second time with shadows off and Soft forced, which the repo's rule that Night never lowers brightness or exposure already guards ([Look-Plan.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Look-Plan.md)).

Night and weather are templates, not scenes. Grow a Garden 2 runs a **10-minute loop (7:30 day, 30-second dusk, about 2 minutes of night)** announced by a HUD timer ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/articles/grow-a-garden-2-day-and-night-cycle-guide)); its rare nights are sky-colour swaps (Normal Moon 69%, Gold 13%, Rainbow 6%, Blood 2%) ([Nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/grow-a-garden-2-night-moons/)) and its weather events last "usually 2 to 5 minutes" with one sky grade, one particle layer, a sound change and a multiplier ([ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/grow-a-garden-2-weather-events-effects-durations/)). Fisch lets players buy weather as single-use totems at 2,000 Tokens each, evidence that selling a one-shot "Rain" through the Peddler is an accepted mechanic ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/fisch-weather-guide/)). The Moonlit Forest reference and the curriculum agree on the one rule for night: light comes from the props, "a night palette of indigo, violet and teal with warm-white and pastel light sources, not a dark scene" ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)), which the planned `NightDresser` should implement as Neon parts with small-range lights on hero props. Ambient life is almost free: terrain grass and clouds drift with GlobalWind, and any ParticleEmitter follows it once `WindAffectsDrag` is on ([Global wind](https://create.roblox.com/docs/environment/global-wind)); the sample's gust script uses a base of (5,0,2) and gusts of (25,0,10) over 3.5 seconds. The real budgets are the mobile ones: "100 per second on mobile" per emitter and Lifetime capped at 20 seconds ([Particle emitters](https://create.roblox.com/docs/effects/particle-emitters)), a community-reported render ceiling of 3,600 particles on phones ([DevForum 573833](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/particle-not-appearing/573833)), and **255 Highlight instances per client**, which makes one outlined wild alien per creature in range affordable ([Highlight class](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Highlight)). Roblox's illustrative scene budget is "below 1,000 draw calls and 1,000,000 triangles" on a baseline device, with the instruction to "choose one baseline device, test on it throughout development" because "Studio's device emulator is not accurate for memory" ([Design for performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/design)); a mid-2024 community budget for 60 FPS on most phones is 500 draw calls and 500,000 triangles ([DevForum 3127146](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3127146)). For this game's island (30 trees at 300 to 900 triangles, 32 species under 1,500) triangles are not the constraint; draw calls and memory are, so the "iPhone SE in the emulator" check needs a second leg on a real low-end phone with the Developer Console open at quality level 3. The plugin set the pros name is stable across the reference video and the DevForum: Stravant's GapFill and ResizeAlign, Brushtool 2.1 for scatter, Redupe for arrays, Archimedes for arcs, Part to Terrain for converting blockouts ([Quenty/PartToTerrainPlugin](https://github.com/Quenty/PartToTerrainPlugin)), Elttob's Atmos skybox browser ([Atmos](https://elttob.itch.io/atmos)) and a Lighting Preset Manager that snapshots the whole Lighting stack with thumbnails ([creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/learn/how-to-use-lighting-preset-manager-roblox-studio)); the last two are worth Tools-Status rows because auditioning a grade in a preset plugin is faster than editing `Lighting.luau` blind.

## Pro UI is a token system plus two painted asset classes

What reads as professional on Roblox in 2026 is a small, consistent system, and most of it is engine primitives. Pet Simulator 99's UI is mostly custom graphics with ImageButtons carrying painted art ([DevForum 2914121](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unique-or-good-looking-ui/2914121)); the genre look is sold as "stud" and "cartoony" packs; the repo's own frame-by-frame read of Steal an Egg records the dialect to the hex: stud header green `#51DF51`, index blue `#68C9FE` to `#89ACFE`, section headings in `#FFEF4A` with a black stroke at about 7% of panel height, a featured egg banner with per-pet odds, rarity-coloured index cells and a green "CLAIM!" ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)). The single most repeated icon rule comes from a solo developer shipping with Claude: "make sure all your icons are black-stroked, thick, or it just doesn't look good", with "large tiles for bundles and big purchases, small tiles for singles". Roblox's own guidelines state the conventions players already know word for word: "Close buttons are always square, red, and contain a white X, appearing only in the top right corner", grey for unusable buttons, a lock over locked features, price in red when unaffordable, and a gold stripe for premium ([UI and UX design](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design)); the UI curriculum adds "display text on top of a contrasting color or with a stroke" and never rely on colour alone ([Choose an art style](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/user-interface-design/choose-an-art-style)). Fredoka One is a built-in engine font (`Enum.Font.FredokaOne`, value 26), so no upload is needed ([Font enum](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/Font)). The primitives that make the look are `UIStroke` (now with `StrokeSizingMode = ScaledSize` and two strokes on one text object), `UICorner` in Scale (0.5 is a pill), `UIGradient` in Linear, Radial or Conical, and the new `UIShadow` with `Inset = true` for an inner lip and a gradient child, flagged beta ([UI appearance modifiers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/appearance-modifiers); [UIStroke](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/UIStroke)). The rarity convention is loose at the bottom and firm from Rare up: Steal a Brainrot uses green for Common, then blue, purple, yellow, red, rainbow, and black-and-white for Secret ([Steal a Brainrot wiki](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Stealing)). The two things code cannot make are the painted 9-slice plates and the rendered glossy icons, and those are precisely what PS99 and Steal an Egg buy from artists: a PS99 icon artist describes work "clear, readable, and visually appealing at small sizes" ([ArtStation](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/AZNZJX)). Freelance listings cluster at 1,000 to 3,000 Robux or about $8 to $75 per frame and **25,000 to 45,000+ Robux (about $120 to $150+) for a full 10+ screen set**, with deposits, rush fees and Robux priced at DevEx rates ([metatablesnow](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/open-metatablesnow-uiux-design-portfolio-2-years-of-experience/3656083); [Showy19](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/showy19-ui-designer-builder-3d-artist/4462562); [UI Project pricing](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ui-project-pricing/3735007)). For a team building UI in code, the economic answer is to commission only those two asset classes, roughly one "frame" each, and keep layout, states and motion in the Builder.

Official numbers are few but the layout rules are firm. Roblox cites the W3C mobile note's **9 x 9 mm touch target** ([Choose an art style](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/user-interface-design/choose-an-art-style); [W3C](https://w3c.github.io/Mobile-A11y-TF-Note/#targetSize)), third-party guides say 44 x 44 px with a larger invisible hit area behind small icons ([creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/blog/roblox-ui-design-best-practices)), and the engine's own floor is "no values lower than 9" for `MinTextSize` ([UITextSizeConstraint](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/UITextSizeConstraint)). Roblox's sample game uses `CornerRadius` of 0.05 to 0.2 in Scale and TextScaled on every text element ([Implement designs in Studio](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/user-interface-design/implement-designs-in-studio)). The rules with teeth: Scale and Offset add, Studio converts a bare `0.5` to Scale and a bare `20` to Offset; "avoid placing important info or virtual buttons" in the bottom corners the thumbstick and jump button occupy; and "a button placed 40% below the screen's top edge is reachable on a phone but almost unreachable on a tablet" ([Position and size](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/position-and-size)). `ScreenGui.ScreenInsets` defaults to `CoreUISafeInsets`, "recommended if the ScreenGui contains interactive UI elements", and `None` behind a notch can hide a button ([On-screen containers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/on-screen-containers)). The player's Text Size setting (`GuiService.PreferredTextSize`, Medium default up to Largest) enlarges every label that has TextScaled off and no UITextSizeConstraint ([Accessibility](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/accessibility)), and developers report "multiple Roblox games now have broken UI" above Medium ([DevForum 3911199](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/preferred-text-scaling-needs-to-be-reverted-abrupt-release-with-no-concerns-addressed-are-disruptive/3911199)). Do not tween a stroke's thickness on text: it "renders and stores many glyph sizes each frame" ([UI appearance modifiers](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/appearance-modifiers)). Motion numbers the tutorials actually give are a 1.08 grow on hover and 0.9 squish on press with a click sound ([TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/roblox-ui-design)) and a 0.2 to 0.5 second range for UI tweens. The repo's Playbook already measures what nobody else publishes (at 667x375 a 14 px Fredoka line needs 17 px of height; a shop tile is 136.9 x 130.4 px; the shop price button floor is 26 px) ([UI-Playbook.md](../docs/vault/05-ui-design/UI-Playbook.md)), but that 26 px floor sits below both the 44 px figure and the 9 mm one (about 57 logical px at 2x on an SE), and every measured fit assumes Text Size at Medium.

Information design in the genre is edges-only and timer-driven. The HUD is a left stack of icon buttons (Shop, Index, Rebirth, Gifts), currency bottom-left, a pet or hotbar along the bottom, an event pill with a countdown and a luck chip, and a Friend Boost readout; everything else is one tap away in a tabbed panel with a red X. Shops lead with a featured timed item showing per-item odds and Robux prices ("9d 15h" on Steal an Egg's Extinction Egg), give bundles the largest tiles ([Dragon Adventures example](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design)), and Grow a Garden's gear shop shows "a timer at the top of the screen" for the next restock ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/roblox/where-to-find-the-gear-shop-in-grow-a-garden)). Every timer on the HUD is a reason to come back, which is why the Playbook's What's-on card and banner priority list are the right shape. Odds presentation is policy, not taste: outcomes and numerical odds must be shown before purchase, as percentages summing to exactly 100%, a worded "Details" affordance is required because "a standalone symbol like the (i) icon without a descriptive word is not sufficient", luck items must state their effect numerically with odds "dynamically updated when these items are active", and `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` gates the whole surface ([Paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)); the worldwide rule is reported to follow South Korea's game law ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319148/20260626/koreas-loot-box-rules-push-roblox-disclose-item-odds-worldwide.htm)). The reveal grammar the genre converges on has four beats: anticipation (a shake that escalates, or taps that each do something), rupture (flash or shards), identity (a silhouette in a ring before the model) and valuation (rarity name in its colour, a percentage, confetti scaled to tier). The owner's own reference shows a Claude-built hatch with "Tap to crack!", "One more!" progress dots, growth per tap, polygon shards, a black silhouette in a ring and a card printing "Void Hydra, SECRET, 3% chance" ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)); the repo's reveal has rupture, identity and valuation and lacks the multi-tap crack and the printed chance line, both policy-friendly. Time per hatch is what the passes sell, which is why the base animation should be worth skipping but never block the next action: in BIG Games' earlier title Auto Hatch cost 150 Robux, Triple Eggs 800 and Egg Skip 95 ([Pet Simulator wiki](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Auto_Hatch_Gamepass_(Pet_Simulator_X)); [Triple Eggs](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Triple_Eggs_Gamepass_(Pet_Simulator_X))), and the systems imitators buy advertise "live odds calculation per egg" with x3 and x8 multi-open passes ([UXR Pet System](https://builtbybit.com/resources/uxr-pet-system.39104/updates)). Particles do not render inside a ViewportFrame, so a reveal that shows the creature in a viewport needs its burst as 2D images ([DevForum 2876100](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/help-with-egg-open-animation/2876100)).

The production methods and the mistakes are two sides of one fact: amateur UI is the absence of a token system. Reviewers across DevForum threads converge on several fonts, more than three colours, gradients on everything, default grey buttons and Source Sans, flat text with no stroke, inconsistent radii and spacing, blurry images, Offset layouts and hover-only affordances on a touch platform ([The ultimate UI design guide](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-ultimate-ui-design-guide/1236916); [rolearn.dev](https://rolearn.dev/insights/mobile-first-design-roblox-engagement)); the moment colours, radii, strokes and spacing come from one module, half of that list cannot be produced by accident, and the other half (blurry images, thin-next-to-thick icons, HUD clutter) needs a review checklist. Two production paths coexist: designers paint in Figma or Photoshop and rebuild in Studio by hand, through 9-slice plates whose guides are set in Studio's editor ([UI 9-slice design](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/9-slice)) or through a new crop of Figma-to-Roblox plugins (roimport, UILint v2, Framewisp, which warns that every uploaded image shows "a striped placeholder" until moderation approves it) ([roimport](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/free-figma-to-roblox-plugin-roimport/4731967); [Framewisp](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/framewisp-%E2%80%94-turn-a-figma-frame-into-native-roblox-ui-in-one-click/4759239)); programmers build in code, optionally through react-lua, Fusion or Vide ([DevForum: React + Roblox](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-react-roblox/2964543)), and since a 20 January 2026 client beta can ship CSS-like StyleSheets with tokens, themes, state selectors and transitions ([UI styling](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/styling); [DevForum 3901480](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/client-beta-you-can-now-publish-styles-in-your-experience/3901480)). StyleSheets are the platform's version of the repo's Theme module; migrating would buy declared hover, press and selected states, and is optional while only one StyleSheet can bind to a tree. The tutorial landscape teaches the look by imitation (recreate PS99, recreate Steal an Egg) and the method by tool (GFXComet's Figma and Photoshop courses, "The Greatest Stud UI Tutorial on Roblox (w/ Figma)") ([GFXComet](https://gfxcomet.gumroad.com/l/academy); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gnIKtS3nUqo)); almost none of it states numbers, which is what makes the measured Playbook unusual. The AI-era variant, taught by cuhfilme in a video that web search cannot find at all, is to load the most popular similar game, screenshot its UI or build one panel yourself first, show Claude, correct until right ("Use Scale, never Offset"), then have Claude save what it learned to the vault; his verdict is that "Claude sucks at making UI for Roblox games unless you know how to do it correctly" ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)). Scored against all of this, the Playbook is right on Scale-only, Fredoka One, outlined text, the red-X rule, rarity colours paired with shapes, bundle tiles and the worded odds line, and it lacks exactly five things the platform now supplies: a stated `CoreUISafeInsets` policy on every interactive ScreenGui, a 44 px hit area behind any face smaller than that, a UITextSizeConstraint or a band sized for Largest on every TextScaled-off label, a thumb-zone rule for the HUD (the Build tray and bottom banners sit where the docs reserve the thumbstick and jump button), and the no-stroke-tween-on-text rule, plus the two reveal beats. A complete device pass in 2026 is four checks, not one: the smallest phone, the largest tablet, a notched phone with insets confirmed, and the same screens with Text Size at Largest and Reduce Motion on; the new Device Simulator groups phones as Flagship, Mid-range and Entry-level and accepts custom devices by width, height and pixel density, so an SE profile can always be added ([Device Simulator](https://create.roblox.com/docs/studio/device-simulator)).

## The vault works when hooks enforce what CLAUDE.md only advises

Every credible 2026 setup for using an Obsidian vault as Claude's project memory converges on one shape, and the team already has it. cuhfilme's vault, the owner's reference, runs numbered folders from `00 Start Here` (Glossary, Home) through `05 UI Design` (a `refs/` folder of named screenshots, UI Checklist, UI Kit, UI Playbook, UI Recipes, User UI Taste), `06 Art Pipelines` (Blender pipeline, importing user art, Open Cloud upload API) and `07` for the game, with a playbook written to the level of hex codes; his claim is "the vault was written by Claude ... starting a new project, it already knows how you like things to look" ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)). Karpathy's "LLM Wiki" gist, which most 2026 vault projects cite, separates immutable `raw/` sources from an LLM-owned `wiki/` and a schema file (`CLAUDE.md` or `AGENTS.md`) that describes structure and workflows, with an `index.md` read first and an append-only `log.md`; it holds "at moderate scale (~100 sources, ~hundreds of pages)" without embeddings, and its line is "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase" ([Karpathy gist](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f)). The game-project variants add a templates folder, decision records with a status field (`Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Superseded`), a frontmatter-driven dashboard, and human-only sections in CLAUDE.md ([msmrfq/Vaulted](https://github.com/msmrfq/Vaulted)); a tiny always-loaded "hot" file of about 120 tokens or 500 words ([obsidian-second-brain](https://github.com/devatsecure/obsidian-second-brain); [Karpathy-LLM-Wiki-Stack](https://github.com/ScrapingArt/Karpathy-LLM-Wiki-Stack)); and the rule "a note without links is a bug" ([obsidian-mind](https://github.com/breferrari/obsidian-mind)). The only non-Obsidian Roblox analogue, ClaudeBlox, reaches the same design from the other side with an `architecture.md` "single source of truth", a bug list, and a "patterns that work, patterns that fail, and MCP gotchas" notes file ([Claudeblox CLAUDE.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Claudeblox/claudeblox/main/CLAUDE.md)). The anti-patterns are as consistent as the pattern: an author who pointed Claude at a 3,214-note vault with no backup found 340 files changed ([Medium](https://medium.com/@trends24/i-pointed-claude-at-my-3-214-note-obsidian-vault-4-months-ago-heres-why-that-was-a-big-mistake-88636e7b8825)); Anthropic's own docs say to "target under 200 lines per CLAUDE.md file" because "bloated CLAUDE.md files cause Claude to ignore your actual instructions" ([Memory docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory); [Best practices](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/best-practices)); and "Your existing directories ARE the wiki", so do not move topic folders into a `wiki/` subfolder and break links ([jhinpan gist](https://gist.github.com/jhinpan/16f240dfce4b45532f28b5df829bc887)). Nothing found quantifies a vault's effect on output; every "it works" claim is an impression.

Five connection routes exist, and only one works for every session this team runs. The plain filesystem (Claude Code opened in or beside the vault) needs no setup, works with Obsidian closed and in cloud sessions, and "Obsidian's file watcher picks those changes up on next launch" ([az9713/claude-code-obsidian](https://github.com/az9713/claude-code-obsidian)). The official Obsidian CLI shipped in v1.12 (February 2026) over IPC, so the app must be open, with `search`, `daily:append`, `backlinks` and `property:set` commands wrapped in Obsidian's own skill pack, maintained by its CEO at **49.6k stars** ([obsidian-cli skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kepano/obsidian-skills/main/skills/obsidian-cli/SKILL.md); [kepano/obsidian-skills](https://github.com/kepano/obsidian-skills)). MCP servers over the Local REST API plugin add heading-targeted PATCH and search by frontmatter, and the plugin now ships its own MCP endpoint at `https://127.0.0.1:27124/mcp/` ([obsidian-local-rest-api](https://github.com/coddingtonbear/obsidian-local-rest-api); [mcp-obsidian](https://github.com/MarkusPfundstein/mcp-obsidian)); folder-reading MCP servers and plugins that embed Claude inside Obsidian round out the set ([iansinnott](https://github.com/iansinnott/obsidian-claude-code-mcp); [Roasbeef](https://github.com/Roasbeef/obsidian-claude-code)). Token cost is set not by the connection but by what gets loaded: in Claude Code's own accounting a project CLAUDE.md costs about 1,800 tokens, MCP tool schemas stay deferred at about 120, and each file read costs 1,100 to 2,400 ([Context window](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/context-window)), and Anthropic's guidance is to keep "lightweight identifiers (file paths ...)" and read on demand ([Effective context engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)). Because the cloud coordinator and build workers have no Obsidian process, the filesystem is the only channel every session shares; the CLI is a Mac-only convenience worth turning on for backlinks and daily-append.

The mature session pattern has moved from instructions to hooks, because instructions get skipped. Anthropic states it plainly: CLAUDE.md instructions are "advisory", "hooks are deterministic and guarantee the action happens", and "to block an action regardless of what Claude decides, use a PreToolUse hook instead" ([Best practices](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/best-practices); [Memory docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory)). obsidian-mind's author tested the alternative and found that without a repo-side instruction "a session made zero vault calls and implemented a design the vault had recorded as explicitly rejected"; its answer is a `SessionStart` hook that injects goals, active work, recent changes and drift flags under a byte budget, a `PostToolUse` hook that validates frontmatter and wikilinks after every `.md` write, and a `Stop` hook that runs a wrap-up checklist ([obsidian-mind](https://github.com/breferrari/obsidian-mind)). Two engine facts make the `compact` matcher necessary: "context that hooks added earlier" is summarised away at compaction while project CLAUDE.md is re-injected from disk, and `SessionStart` hooks matching `compact` run again ([Context window](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/context-window); [Hooks guide](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks-guide)). Path-scoped `.claude/rules/*.md` files with `paths:` frontmatter load only when Claude touches matching files ([Memory docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory)), which is the mechanism for "read the UI Playbook before UI work". Every serious lint is read-only and leaves fixes for review: RoseCompanion checks broken and ambiguous links, orphans, duplicates, missing properties and stale notes (default 180 days) and "never modifies a note" ([vault-health-check](https://github.com/RoseCompanion/obsidian-vault-health-check)); AgriciDaniel's wiki-lint says "Never auto-fix a lint result" ([wiki-lint](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-obsidian/blob/main/skills/wiki-lint/SKILL.md)); and one author argues a script must verify links because "you can't trust an agent's self-reporting on link integrity" ([eferro](https://www.eferro.net/2026/04/how-i-use-claude-code-to-maintain.html)). Daily notes are "journals rather than knowledge: append-only, with open items carried forward" ([sphelps](https://sphelps.substack.com/p/a-shared-memory-for-claude-code)), and the az9713 skills timestamp each entry as a heading with a Related line of wikilinks ([workflows](https://github.com/az9713/claude-code-obsidian/blob/main/claude-code-obsidian-workflows.md)).

For two people on separate Claude accounts with a cloud coordinator, a Mac session and workers, git is the only memory channel, and Claude's native features confirm it rather than replace it. Auto memory "is machine-local ... Files are not shared across machines or cloud environments" and skips "anything your CLAUDE.md files already say" ([Memory docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory)); cross-session messaging reaches only your own sessions, a message "can't approve anything" and "can't change configuration", and a container session and a host session cannot reach each other ([Cross-session messaging](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cross-session-messaging)); cloud sessions clone "your current directory's GitHub remote at your current branch, not your local checkout, so push first" ([Claude Code in the cloud](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-code-on-the-web)); Claude Projects memory is per-project and separate from repo CLAUDE.md, with the advice to "put instructions about a repository in its CLAUDE.md and notes about the project in project memory" ([Claude Projects](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-projects)). The one team vault found shares through Syncthing with git kept on a single automation host ("A vault is not a git checkout") and "exactly one owner" per note ([obsidian-team-vault](https://github.com/SimonSkade1/obsidian-team-vault)); a VPS-plus-laptop user learned to exclude `.git` from sync after two machines corrupted the index ([SSD Nodes](https://www.ssdnodes.com/learn/claude-code-with-obsidian-vault)). The claude-mem plugin already enabled in this repo stores observations in SQLite and Chroma and has no markdown or Obsidian output ([claude-mem](https://github.com/thedotmack/claude-mem)), so it is per-Mac transcript memory, not team memory. Against this field the repo already has the hard parts: the vault inside the repo in cuhfilme's layout, a 51-line CLAUDE.md that points at it, hub notes, frontmatter, a glossary, a measured Playbook with refs, a decisions table, a per-day log with per-session headings, a standard-library lint that fails CI on broken links, Board-based claiming and "git is the sync channel" ([CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md); [docs/vault/README.md](../docs/vault/README.md); [tools/lint_vault.py](../tools/lint_vault.py); [Handoff.md](../docs/vault/00-start-here/Handoff.md)). What it lacks, ranked by value, is a checked-in `hooks` block (a `SessionStart` digest on `startup|resume|compact`, `PostToolUse` running `lint_vault.py` after any vault write, a `Stop` hook that refuses to end when `src/` changed and the log did not), a 300 to 500 word `Now.md` so sessions stop paying for the 52 KB Handoff on every start, path-scoped rules for `src/client/**`, `src/shared/data/**` and `assets/models/**`, a templates folder and decision records with `status`, read-only lint additions (ambiguous links, duplicate names, staleness, a `--strict` flag), timestamped log headings with one file per session per day when two sessions overlap, and a written stance that auto memory is personal scratch and claude-mem is finished (install `bun`) or dropped ([Tools-Status.md](../docs/vault/02-how-we-work/Tools-Status.md)).

## Script the creature build; use the Blender MCP only to look

The Blender MCP in October 2026 is `mcp-for-blender` (formerly `blender-mcp`), at **30,866 stars**, pushed 6 October, MIT, added to Claude Code with `claude mcp add blender uvx mcp-for-blender` ([GitHub search](https://api.github.com/search/repositories?q=blender+mcp&sort=stars&per_page=20); [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahujasid/blender-mcp/main/README.md)). Its nine tools are `execute_blender_code`, `look`, `get_scene_info`, `generate_3d`, `search_assets`, `import_asset`, `get_addon_status`, `disable_telemetry` and `record_trajectory_feedback`; `look` offers `viewport`, `camera`, an `angles` turnaround of up to six auto-framed views, and a `frames` strip over an animation, in solid, material, rendered ("EEVEE and Workbench only"), wireframe or x-ray shading at a default 768 px; `generate_3d` makes "one object per call: not a whole scene, the ground, or parts to assemble", "can cost the user money", takes 1 to 3 minutes and "arrives at arbitrary scale and facing" ([server.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender/main/src/blender_mcp/server.py)). The socket "has no authentication or encryption", a safe mode blocks file and network access from scripts, and the README says "ALWAYS save your work before using" code execution ([README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahujasid/blender-mcp/main/README.md)). The one thing no MCP, fork or script can do is sculpt: Blender's API docs explain that operators "check for the active area type", failing with "context is incorrect" ([info_gotchas_operators](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blender/blender/main/doc/python_api/rst/info_gotchas_operators.rst)), and blenderwright, the most complete fork at 191 tools including sculpt setup, armatures and auto weights, states "Sculpt strokes cannot be simulated" and needs the GUI open ([blenderwright](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HoldMyBeer-gg/blenderwright/main/README.md)). Skill packs now exist that run Blender headless with Roblox export and asset QA skills ([EL4CTEO/blender-skills](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EL4CTEO/blender-skills/main/README.md)) or 94 skills including creature-artist and low-poly styles with no Roblox export ([arjun988/blender-skills](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arjun988/blender-skills/main/README.md)).

No public, reproducible Claude-driven cute-creature pipeline exists. Every 2026 "Claude plus Blender for Roblox" project on GitHub stops short: `3D-Asset-Agent` runs headless Blender with Studio's MCP and a clean-up chain (`merge_by_distance`, `recalc_normals`, `keep_largest`, `decimate`, `remesh`, `bake_texture` to one 1024 px map) but generates with Hunyuan3D-2 on an NVIDIA GPU with 6 GB+ (about 16 GB for texturing) and reports that "native Claude was still faster and cheaper" in its own benchmark ([3D-Asset-Agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CodeByJN/3D-Asset-Agent/main/README.md)); Roqer is Windows-only with macOS "untested" and uses Blender only for "curved shapes and vehicle bodies" ([Roqer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/S4US/Roqer/main/README.md)); the Genkidama pipeline does R6 animation and VFX in "2-5 feedback rounds" where "the agent does roughly 85-95% of the work" ([genkidama](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bikotoru/roblox-ai-genkidama-pipeline/main/README.md)). The earliest first-person account is an April 2025 post using Claude Desktop with the Blender MCP and an upload plugin ([X](https://x.com/StevenDarlow/status/1907302606638080083)). The creators the team follows do not use Blender through Claude at all: lihfolk chose "Claude Design" over Blender because driving Blender through Claude "uses far more tokens for a minimal difference in result", and cuhfilme's vault has a Blender Pipeline note covering headless runs and FBX settings but shows no close creature ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)). The route the repo already runs is therefore the only one that is fully scriptable, free, deterministic and versioned: a written recipe per species, headless `bpy` building one fused skin, a reduction to budget, and materials restored from data after upload. Its measured numbers are the strongest first-hand evidence in this report: metaballs to mesh, Smooth, Decimate took **17,984 raw triangles to 2,000 in 0.2 s** as "one blended skin"; voxel remesh plus QuadriFlow at 1,000 faces gave 1,750 triangles in 1.1 s; a Skin modifier on a 12-vertex stick skeleton plus two subdivisions gave 2,888; a build is 1 to 3 s and a lit render about 20 s, so a tuning loop is 1 to 2 minutes; metaballs keep hooves and antler tines where voxel remesh "erodes them" and are three to four times faster; QuadriFlow refuses non-manifold polygonised skins and is the one to use only if the mesh will be rigged ([Character-Pipeline.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Character-Pipeline.md); [Sculpt-Pass.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Sculpt-Pass.md)). The exportable "fluid" toolkit is narrow and worth stating: a fused volume, voxel remesh only when hard edges or manifoldness are needed, Decimate otherwise, a Bevel modifier for chunky hard parts, Smooth by Angle plus Weighted Normal written as custom normals into the GLB, and colour as vertex colour or a small palette; the Bevel shader node is "Cycles Only", "changes only the shading, not the geometry", and never reaches Roblox ([bevel.rst](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dfelinto/blender-manual/main/manual/render/shader_nodes/input/bevel.rst)). A workable live session is seven moves: `get_addon_status` and `get_scene_info`, load the species sheet, run the checked-in builder, `look` with `angles` at 512 px, critique against the sheet's three checks, change one parameter per pass for at most three passes, then the four hand fixes that always recur (thin parts blunted or split off, accessory placement, eye and mouth sinking, triangle budget); the repo's 14 October look pass on three event species took about 40 minutes and three iterations in exactly this shape ([2026-10-14 log](../docs/vault/08-log/2026-10-14.md)).

Generation services are a form explorer for a few hero species, not a roster pipeline, and the cheapest sensible one is Tripo through its own API. The add-on source settles what the Blender MCP actually requests: the Rodin call sends `tier: "Sketch"` and `mesh_mode: "Raw"` and imports a GLB with "no scale, orientation, or normalisation"; Tripo "is only offered through Premium"; Hunyuan3D goes through Tencent Cloud with `quality` Premium-only ([addon.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender/main/addon.py)). Tripo's v3 API, read through an MCP wrapper, has a dedicated low-poly model `P1-20260311`, a `retopologize` tool with `targetFaces` and `quad`, format conversion to FBX and glTF, rigging and preset-animation retargeting; one observer logged **40 credits for a P1 low-poly text-to-3D in 76 seconds** and 50 for the detailed model ([trident-mcp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mordor-forge/trident-mcp/main/docs/MCP_TOOLS.md)), and its Professional plan is reported at $19.90 a month for 3,000 credits with private commercial output ([fitgap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/058341/tripo-studio)). Rodin's Creator plan is reported at $24 to $30 a month and free users "pay only when they confirm results" ([hyper3d.ai](https://hyper3d.ai/pricing)); Hunyuan3D 2.1 needs "10 GB VRAM for shape generation, 21GB for texture" ([Hunyuan3D-2.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/main/README.md)) and its 2.0 licence excludes the EU, UK and South Korea ([Character-Pipeline.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Character-Pipeline.md)). Free-tier commercial rights are disputed between Tripo's own blog and third parties ([Tripo blog](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/commercial-use-ai-3d-models)), so the rule is to ship nothing from a free tier without reading the current terms. Roblox's policy is unambiguous on responsibility: "You are responsible for the content delivered to users, even if it is AI-generated" ([generative-AI.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/generative-AI.md)), and every upload passes moderation "generally ... within a few hours" ([assets](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/projects/assets/index.md)). The "best for cute" question cannot be settled from text; it needs one species prompt run through Tripo P1, Meshy and Rodin and judged on the three ship checks, about 10 to 15 USD of credits.

Animation needs its own route, and none of them is "generate it in the viewer". A creature rig without a Humanoid needs "an `AnimationController` with a child `Animator`" ([animation/using.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/animation/using.md)); bones become `Bone` objects, "a vertex can not be influenced by more than 4 bones or joints", and the root takes no influences ([specifications.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/art/modeling/specifications.md); [rigging.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/art/modeling/rigging.md)); the Importer reads rigs and animation data from `.fbx` and `.gltf` with Rig Type Custom ([importer.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/studio/importer.md)); Blender's FBX export should enable Bake Animation only for keyframed content ([export-requirements.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/art/modeling/export-requirements.md)); and the Animation Editor's "Publish to Roblox" is what yields an asset id ([animation/editor.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/animation/editor.md)). Open Cloud accepts the Animation asset type only as `.rbxm` or `.rbxmx`, so every animation publish goes through Studio ([usage-assets.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud/guides/usage-assets.md)), and Studio's glTF export "does not support animation data" ([gltf-export.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/art/modeling/gltf-export.md)). The Claude animation skills and Cautioned's "Blender Animations" plugin, where "bones created in Blender become Motor6Ds in Studio", are built for R6 and R15 humanoids and are the wrong tool for a quadruped or blob ([Cautioned](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Cautioned/Blender-Animations-Plugin/main/README.md)). The cheapest coherent plan for 35 creatures with no artist is the one the references use: no skinned rig at launch, idle, walk and hatch as tweened MeshPart motion and VFX from a data table, QuadriFlow topology and at most four influences kept so a rig can be added later, and Tripo's `rig_model` and `retarget_animation` tried only on hero species. Four doors lead into Studio, each for a different step: the Importer once per new build to see warnings and the SurfaceAppearance result; the Open Cloud Assets API for the batch (GLB or FBX up to 20 MB, a Model of MeshParts, `moderationState` in the Operation response), which the repo used for 53 models with colours restored from `materials.json` ([Map-Dressing.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Map-Dressing.md)); the official Roblox Blender plugin, an MIT "reference implementation" that uploads selected objects as packages after a browser login and adds nothing to a scripted pipeline ([roblox-blender-plugin](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/roblox-blender-plugin/main/README.md)); and the built-in Studio MCP with `insert_asset` by id, `generate_mesh`, `upload_image`, `screen_capture`, `execute_luau` and a `subagent` tool for `explore` and `playtest` ([studio/mcp.md](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/studio/mcp.md)), which replaces the deprecated `studio-rust-mcp-server` ([README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/studio-rust-mcp-server/main/README.md)). The grey-import problem is universal (vos_studiostt's GLBs arrived as "gray silhouettes") and the repo's `materials.json` loader is the same fix the creators write as ReplicatedStorage modules. The vault corrections that fall out of this are small: the project rename, the Rodin Sketch-tier settings, `look`'s EEVEE-only rendered mode (review renders should use `preview.py`), and the sculpt limitation now sourced rather than remembered.

## Claude Design draws the review; Blender and Luau ship it

Two different products are called "Claude Design" in the references, and only one of them has a future. Anthropic Labs launched Claude Design as a standalone site on **17 April 2026**, moved it into every Claude conversation on 16 September, and took Docs, Slides and Design out of beta "on every plan, including Free" on 8 October, with "more than 45 million docs, decks, and designs" made since launch ([release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes); [Dashboards and Motion](https://claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion)). The standalone site "remains available until December 14, 2026"; design systems migrate but projects do not, chats "are deleted after it closes", comments are not carried over, and public links to standalone projects "will no longer work" ([Migrate from standalone Claude Design](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17440474-migrate-from-standalone-claude-design-to-claude)). The standalone app is where the creators' workflow lives: vos_studiostt's footage shows "Model a 3D object", star ratings, a reference image and a `land_shark.glb` download, outputs as browser pages (`Void+Hydra.html`), and a two-prompt iteration whose second prompt lists changes and names which part of the reference to copy ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)). No first-party page documents a 3D mode, star ratings, GLB export or reference-image handling; a help-centre search for "3D object" returns nothing relevant ([help-center search](https://support.claude.com/en/?q=3D%20object)). The in-chat product is an artboard canvas: the Design type describes itself as "live artboards laid out on a canvas" for screens, mockups, wireframes and posters, each a self-contained HTML page, phone app artboards at 390x844, uploads limited to images and fonts, external loads limited to Google Fonts ([Design artifact type](https://claude.ai/artifact/QKN21svewxgyPb6SYRqWnd)); its exports are .zip, PDF, PPTX, Google Slides, standalone HTML, "Send to other tools" (Miro, Netlify, Vercel, v0 among them) and "Handoff to Claude Code" ([Get started](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14604416-get-started-with-claude-design)). From Claude Code, `/design [brief]` publishes "artboards on one canvas" and "you can export each artboard as PNG or PDF", requiring v2.1.265 or later per the commands reference (the week-34 digest said v2.1.234) ([commands](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/commands); [week 34](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/whats-new/2026-w34)). `/design-sync` converts "your repo's React design system" and does not apply to Luau ([commands](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/commands)); instead Claude Code artifacts read a design-system block from CLAUDE.md or a theme file and treat it "as higher precedence than its own choices" ([Claude Code artifacts](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/artifacts)). Claude "doesn't generate photos or illustrations the way image-generation tools do"; its visuals are HTML and SVG ([Can Claude produce images?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9002504-can-claude-produce-images)), so an artboard is code, not a bitmap, and raster look comes only from uploaded images. Claude Motion, the animation sibling, exports MP4 but is "in beta on Team and Enterprise" only ([Motion](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17454997-get-started-with-claude-motion); [pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). Design "counts toward the same shared usage limits as the rest of Claude, including Claude Code", and "full decks or designs use more than a typical message" ([Get started](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14604416-get-started-with-claude-design); [product page](https://claude.com/product/design)); lihfolk's budget note is that ten generated enemies "used about 30% of the usage allowance" ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)).

What the creators actually got from the 3D mode argues against it even before the closing date. vos_studiostt's first pass was "a grey two-legged shark with red glowing tail", the second "a bigger, fanged shark-dragon with red-lit blades", and his verdict was "there are still some imperfections" and "the water animations are kind of janky"; lihfolk's in-game enemy is "primitives plus glow, not a smooth skin", with his own caveat that "making things ready for the public is on you" ([Character-Pipeline.md](../docs/vault/06-art-pipelines/Character-Pipeline.md)). The GLB path is: download, rename to the exact species name, File > Import, and accept a grey, untextured model with no animation; colours and effects are then rebuilt by Claude-written ReplicatedStorage modules, and "all 63 of the animals in our game were animated completely with 3 or 4 prompts" in chat, which means script-driven motion, not keyframe clips in the file ([media/tiktok/NOTES.md](../media/tiktok/NOTES.md)). Nobody in the set shows a fluid cute single skin from the tool; the creatures that look good are good because of the coat on top (emissive accents, a ring, particles), the viewer creatures are dark and menacing where the alien roster must stay cute, and the only GLB source closes in eight weeks. The creator's "handoff document" is a context-window workaround for the standalone app's long single-project chats, "a new conversation with an entire script for everything that you've done"; in the in-chat product the same job is done by a design-system block in CLAUDE.md, a Design System artifact, and the repo's own `Handoff.md`, so the team needs the habit (every Design session starts from the same written brief) and not the mechanism. The structure the best results came from, in the creator's order, is a context script, the species request "with the previous details in mind", a reference image with an explicit instruction about which part to copy, a second prompt as a numbered list of deltas, and a rating per output; steps three and four are what he credits for "a thousand times better".

The right use is as the review surface before code, and the table below is where each tool sits.

The first week, each step one change: confirm on the Mac that `claude --version` meets v2.1.265 and `/design` appears while signed in, change the Tools-Status row from "chosen instead" to "in use for mockups and comps; never for meshes" and note the 14 December closing in it ([Tools-Status.md](../docs/vault/02-how-we-work/Tools-Status.md)); add a design-system block to CLAUDE.md or a `Design-Tokens.md` it points at, carrying the Theme hexes, Fredoka One, rarity colours, radii and gloss numbers, since that is what `/design` reads; write a one-page brief in `docs/vault/05-ui-design/` (the game in two lines, the section 15 art rules, the Playbook rules, the token table, device sizes, what was rated well, the deliverable) and attach it to every session; then `/design` the two or three screens Ethan rejected, since the launch slipped on his verdict "I'm not satisfied with launching the game right now given the designs and UI of the game" ([Board.md](../docs/vault/00-start-here/Board.md)), three options each at 390x844 with Fredoka from Google Fonts, outlined text and gloss bands in CSS, no glass, exporting PNGs to the Playbook refs and letting a Sonnet worker rebuild the chosen screen in Luau from the artboard and the token table, never from its HTML; then a species canvas with one artboard per Tier-1 species using uploaded clay turnarounds, whose greyscale silhouette strip is the design doc's legibility check and whose outcome feeds the melt recipes ([GAME_DESIGN.md](../docs/GAME_DESIGN.md)); then three thumbnail artboards and the icon close-up from `store_renders.py` output; then an interactive hatch-reveal artboard (multi-tap crack with dots, growth per tap, shards, silhouette in a ring, tier card with the chance line, "Tap to continue") with sliders, whose chosen numbers go into a data table under `src/shared/data` for the day-7 Epic egg. The prompts follow the creators' pattern under the team's rules: "Before drawing, ask every question you need about the content of the screen", and for the second pass, "Change only these: 1 ... 2 ... keep everything else exactly as in the current artboard. From the reference image copy only the ear shape, nothing of its colours." What it must not be used for is equally clear: any 3D mesh that ships or anything started in the standalone app; rigged animation export, since the GLB carries none; raster art (icons, textures, sprites), since Claude does not paint and the bar is a rendered Blender icon; shipped UI, since the Luau builder with Scale sizing is the only UI source; and a design-system source of truth, since `Theme.luau` stays the truth and the CLAUDE.md block is a copy regenerated when it changes.

## Conclusion

The six strands of this research reduce to one principle the top games apply everywhere: a single rule applied without exception, then a last pass that is cheap and visible. Creatures are one cube grown by one method and recoloured by rarity; maps are a greybox finished by an edge, a path and local light; UI is a token module finished by outlines, gloss and two painted asset classes. Nothing in the pipelines of Adopt Me, PS99, Grow a Garden or Fisch is generated, and the one place generation entered the genre (Steal a Brainrot's source images) it entered as a liability. The team's instinct to melt its own recipes rather than generate is therefore not a workaround for lacking an artist; it is the method the studios use, run by a script instead of a person. The genuinely new thing the Claude-era creators demonstrate is not any tool but a loop that turns corrections into written standards, and the research shows that loop fails silently when it is advisory and holds when a hook enforces it, which makes the checked-in `hooks` block the highest-value change the vault can take.

Two deadlines and one measurement gap should shape the next block. Claude Design's standalone 3D mode is a dead end by 14 December 2026, so the sunk cost of chasing it is zero and the in-chat canvas should be adopted now for the four review jobs where it is genuinely faster than code. Roblox's lighting API has already moved under the team's `Lighting.luau`, and the phone reality (Soft lighting and no shadows forced on low quality) means every night and weather look must pass a second time in the degraded state on a real device, because the emulator cannot show it. Finally, the "best for cute" question for generators, the only place a small spend could change the plan, is unanswered by any source and costs about 15 USD to answer with one species through Tripo P1, Meshy and Rodin judged on the three ship checks; until that trial runs, the melted roster is both the safer and the better-evidenced choice.
