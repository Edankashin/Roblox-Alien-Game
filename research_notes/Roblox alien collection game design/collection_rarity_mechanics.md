## Key Question 1: What rarity tier structures and odds are standard in top Roblox collection games, and how steep are the value multipliers between tiers?

### Takeaway
Top Roblox collection games converge on 5–8 named tiers (Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic/Secret/Exclusive), with per-tier drop odds that fall from ~30–50% at Common to 0.1–1% at the top "named" tier and then to astronomically small "Secret" odds (1 in 200,000 up to 1 in 1,000,000,000). Value does not scale linearly with tier: income/damage/sale-value multipliers between adjacent tiers are typically 3–10x, and across the full ladder are 4–6 orders of magnitude, with variant/mutation systems (Gold, Rainbow, Shiny, Huge, mutations) layering further 1.25x–365x multipliers on top.

### Inferences
- The "standard" Roblox ladder is now 6–8 tiers, and the recent hits (Steal a Brainrot, BGSI, Pet Catchers) all add one or more tiers above "Legendary" (Mythic, Brainrot God, Secret, OG, Celestial, Infinity) whose odds are quoted as "1 in N" rather than percentages. This is a deliberate presentation choice: "1 in 50,000,000" reads as more prestigious than "0.000002%".
- Tier-to-tier multipliers of roughly 3–10x on the primary value stat (income/s, damage, sale price) appear to be the norm; the full ladder spans about 10^6–10^9. Common pets therefore do not stay competitive on raw stats; games keep them relevant through fusion/crafting sinks (see KQ3), not through balance.
- Variant/mutation overlays are consistently *smaller* than tier jumps for the always-available variants (Gold 1.2–1.25x, Shiny 1.5–1.85x, Diamond 1.5x) and only reach tier-sized multipliers for rare or event-limited variants (Rainbow 10x in SaB, Rainbow x50 in GaG, Shocked x100). This lets the base tier remain the dominant signal of value while variants create "same creature, better copy" chase.
- Grow a Garden's additive-inside-the-bracket formula is a notable anti-runaway design: ten x2 mutations give x11, not x1024, while still letting one or two headline mutations (x100+) dominate. Steal a Brainrot's fully multiplicative stacking is the opposite approach and produces the $1B/s "OG" ceiling.

## Key Question 2: How do games make rare finds feel rewarding (visual, audio, social, and progression signals)?

### Takeaway
The reliably documented signals are: (1) a visible status change on the creature itself (size, glow, color cycling, "????" stats, ridability), (2) server-scope announcements for the rarest spawns/hatches, (3) purchasable or earned luck meters that make the player feel they are "working the odds," and (4) tying the rare find to a leveling/evolution track so the moment of acquisition is the start of a progression arc rather than its end.

- Real-money secondary demand is itself a reward signal: eBay listings exist for PS99 Huge pets and "101x Shiny Rainbow Hell Scorpion," BGSI "All Secret Shiny Mythic Pets," and Grow a Garden "Mutation Pets (Rainbow, Mega, Shiny)." — [eBay: PS99 Huge pets](https://www.ebay.de/itm/204646010312); [eBay: BGSI secret shiny mythic](https://www.ebay.de/itm/405799030915); [eBay: GaG mutation pets](https://www.ebay.de/itm/388774750232)

### Inferences
- Across these games the rare tier is made legible to *other players* first (size, glow, server announcement, "????" stats), and to the owner second. The design intent is social proof: a Huge or Mega Neon is a wearable flex in a shared world, which is why so many signals are visual rather than numeric.
- Luck boosts are as much a psychological device as a probability change: showing a "Lucky %" meter (BGSI Debug) or a server-wide luck tier converts a pure RNG event into something the player feels they influenced, which cushions long dry streaks.
- Scheduled rare events (Witching Hour every 2h; Community Day) convert rarity from a private slot-machine into a shared appointment, which is likely the highest-retention form of rarity reveal because it recurs on a calendar.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve primary descriptions of PS99's or BGSI's hatch animation sequence (egg shake, screen flash, rarity reveal order) or whether Huge/Titanic hatches are broadcast game-wide vs. server-only. This is commonly reported anecdotally but was not confirmed in a citable source this session.
- No source retrieved documents the audio design (jingles, sparkle SFX) for shiny/gleaming reveals in Pokémon GO or Loomian Legacy; the Pokémon GO sparkle animation is widely known but was not confirmed in a fetched page.
- No quantitative study linking reveal effects to retention was found.

## Key Question 3: How do games balance "rarer = faster/more income" against the risk that rare creatures make common ones obsolete?

### Takeaway
Nobody solves this with balance; they solve it with sinks and relative scaling. The three documented patterns are (a) duplicate-sink fusion/evolution that converts piles of commons into one rarer unit (PS99 Fuse/Rainbow/Garg machines, Adopt Me Neon/Mega Neon, Palworld condensing), (b) relative scaling so the top tier is defined as a multiple of *your* current best rather than a fixed number (PS99 Huge/Titanic), and (c) role specialization where "lesser" creatures do something the rare one does not (Grow a Garden pet abilities, Palworld work suitability). Mastery gates and slot caps throttle how fast rare multipliers can be stacked.

### Cited Findings
- **Fusion as a duplicate sink (PS99):** the Fuse Machine combines 3–100 pets into one stronger pet; Golden and Rainbow pets can result from fusing but Shiny cannot. The Rainbow Machine requires a minimum of 10 identical Golden pets to make one Rainbow. The Garg Machine uses a point system where Gold pets add 1 point, Rainbow 2, Shiny 3. — [Pet Simulator Wiki: Fuse Machine](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Fuse_Machine_(Pet_Simulator_99)); [Pet Simulator Wiki: Rainbow Machine](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Rainbow_Machine_(Pet_Simulator_99)); [Pet Simulator Wiki: Garg Machine](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Garg_Machine_(Pet_Simulator_99))
- **Relative scaling (PS99):** Huge damage is X × (1 + Y + Z) with X = the player's strongest regular pet, and Titanics are "3x stronger than the player's best pets." The Best Friend enchant guarantees a Huge "will always be stronger than your best pets." — [Pet Simulator Wiki: Huge Pets](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Huge_Pets_(Pet_Simulator_99)); [Pet Simulator Wiki: Titanic Pets](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Titanic_Pets_(Pet_Simulator_99))
- **Evolution via duplicates (Adopt Me):** a Neon requires four copies of the same pet raised to Full Grown and combined in the Neon Cave; a Mega Neon requires four Luminous (fully-aged) Neons of the same species, i.e., 16 base copies. — [Adopt Me Wiki: Mega Neons](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Mega_Neons); [RPGStash: how to make Neon pets](https://www.rpgstash.com/blog/adopt-me-how-to-make-neon-pets)
- **Role specialization (Grow a Garden pets):** pets have timed abilities rather than flat multipliers — Dragonfly applies Gold, Butterfly applies Rainbow, Scarlet Macaw "every 8 minutes has a 16% chance to apply the Verdant mutation to a nearby fruit," Eggnog Chick reduces hatch time and applies Eggnog mutation, Sea Turtle grants bonus XP to a random pet and can Wet a fruit, and a Divine pet "activates every 16 minutes with a 15% chance of applying the Voidtouched mutation." — [Pocket Tactics: all GaG pets](https://www.pockettactics.com/grow-a-garden-pet); [Sportskeeda: Divine pets](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/all-divine-pets-grow-garden); [gagdata: Rainbow guide](https://www.gagdata.com/mutations/rainbow)
- Grow a Garden's crop-value formula caps the benefit of mutation spam: environmental mutations add inside one bracket (1 + Σ − N), so stacking many small mutations is linear, not exponential. — [gagdata: stacking guide](https://www.gagdata.com/mutations/stacking)
- **Mastery gates (BGSI):** Shiny hatching is locked until Doggy Level 2 Pets Mastery and Mythic until Doggy Level 6 / Dark Arts Mastery, so variant multipliers (1.5x/1.75x/2.625x) are paced by progression rather than available from minute one. — [BGSI Wiki: Pets](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Pets)
- **Multiplicative stacking without caps (Steal a Brainrot):** mutations and traits "stack multiplicatively with each other, meaning a brainrot with both a high mutation and strong traits can reach income numbers far beyond what either bonus alone would suggest." — [GameRant: traits and multipliers](https://gamerant.com/roblox-steal-a-brainrot-all-traits-multipliers/)
- **Pal condensing as a duplicate sink (Palworld 1.0):** reaching 1/2/3/4 stars costs 4/8/12/24 duplicate pals (48 total); each star adds +7 Work Speed, star 1 adds +1 to the pal's best suitability, star 2 its second-best, star 3 its third-best, and star 4 +1 to every suitability. Natural suitability runs 1–8 and can be pushed to 10. — [Palworld Wiki: Pal Essence Condenser](https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Pal_Essence_Condenser); [palworldcalc: condensing calculator](https://palworldcalc.com/condensing-calculator/); [Palmods: work suitability 1.0](https://www.palmods.gg/guides/whats-new/work-suitability)
- **Passive-skill layer (Palworld):** Artisan +50% Work Speed (Rank 3), Work Slave +30% Work Speed / −30% Attack (Rank 1), Serious +20%; combined with Remarkable Craftsmanship the practical total is ~+175%, and buffs stack multiplicatively with food, building and Pal Soul bonuses. — [Palworld Wiki: Artisan](https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Artisan); [Physgun: passive skills list](https://physgun.com/tools/palworld-passive-skills/); [Godlike: best passives](https://godlike.host/best-passive-skills-in-palworld-en-blog/)
- **Pity systems (Cookie Run: Kingdom):** an Epic is guaranteed every 100 draws and event Legendary/Ancient gachas guarantee the featured cookie after 250 draws. — [Cookie Run: Kingdom Wiki: Gacha](https://cookierunkingdom.fandom.com/wiki/Gacha)
- **Spec/investment value (Creatures of Sonaria):** a fully spec'd creature "can be worth 40–70% more mush than an unspec'd copy," so investment in a creature, not just its tier, carries value. — [game.guide: CoS value list](https://www.game.guide/creatures-of-sonaria-value-list)
- **Negative mutations (Fisch):** Dirty 0.3x and Exploded 0.1x exist alongside positive ones, so "mutation" is not synonymous with "better." — [Bloxodes: Fisch mutations](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/fisch/mutations)

| Anti-obsolescence lever | Game(s) | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| Fusion sink | PS99 | 3–100 pets → 1 stronger; 10 Golden → 1 Rainbow |
| Evolution sink | Adopt Me | 4 copies → Neon; 4 Neon → Mega Neon |
| Condensing sink | Palworld | 48 duplicates → 4-star, +7 work speed/star, +1 suitability |
| Relative scaling | PS99 | Huge = f(your best pet); Titanic = 3x your best |
| Role abilities | Grow a Garden, Palworld | Pet applies specific mutation; Pal has specific suitabilities |
| Additive stacking | Grow a Garden | 1 + Σ − N bracket |
| Mastery gating | BGSI | Shiny/Mythic locked behind pet mastery levels |
| Pity | Cookie Run: Kingdom | Epic/100 draws; featured/250 draws |

### Inferences
- The pattern that most directly answers "several creatures do the same job but rarer ones do it faster" is a hybrid: Roblox income games use pure multipliers at the tier level *and* sink commons into rares via fusion, while farming/builder games (Grow a Garden pets, Palworld) attach distinct abilities so a common unit can be the only one that performs a given job. Both approaches appear in the top performers; neither is standard alone.
- Relative scaling (PS99 Huge/Titanic) is the cleanest fix for power creep across hundreds of content updates: the rare item's value is permanent because it is defined as a multiple of whatever the player currently fields.
- Confirmed pity systems are a mobile-gacha convention (Cookie Run: Kingdom). No Roblox title in this set was confirmed to have a hard pity; they substitute luck boosts, mastery progression and purchasable server luck. This is a meaningful design-culture difference worth deciding on explicitly for a new game.

### Gaps
- Whether Adopt Me rarity confers any gameplay power (versus being purely cosmetic/trading value) was not confirmed from a fetched source; widely understood to be cosmetic-only but unverified here.
- Team/equip size caps (PS99 slots, BGSI slots, Grow a Garden pet slots, Steal a Brainrot base slots/floors) were not retrieved.
- No Roblox pity-system confirmation or denial was found for PS99, BGSI, Grow a Garden or Steal a Brainrot.
- Cookie Run: Kingdom's soulstone/promotion system and how Epic vs. Legendary cookies compare in the meta were not retrieved.

## Key Question 4: How is exploration-based discovery designed vs. egg/gacha hatching, and which drives more engagement/retention?

### Takeaway
Exploration-driven games gate rarity by *where/when/how* you look (Fisch: location, rod, bait, enchant; Loomian Legacy: wild encounters and rally chains; Pokémon GO: time-boxed events and raids; Palworld: suitability-specific pals in specific biomes), while hatching games gate it by *volume* (eggs per hour) and purchased luck. The 2025–2026 Roblox hits mostly chose hybrids — physical conveyor spawns you can see and steal (Steal a Brainrot), throwable catch-cubes on world spawns (Pet Catchers), timed eggs with pets that then alter the world (Grow a Garden). No reliable head-to-head retention data was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Fisch (exploration):** rarity is layered by rod/bait/enchant: mutations like Ascended (Masterline Rod, 25%), Royal (Royal Spear, 35%) and Jackpot (Random Rod, 50%) are tied to specific gear, and Shiny/Sparkling (1%) and Albino (~0.652%) can be boosted via Blessed/Noir enchants. — [Bloxodes: Fisch mutations](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/fisch/mutations); [Beebom: Fisch mutations](https://beebom.com/all-fisch-mutations/); [Droid Gamers: Fisch mutation odds](https://www.droidgamers.com/guides/fisch-mutation-guide/)
- **Loomian Legacy (exploration + chaining):** gleamings are wild encounters at 1/4096 base; the Rally Ranch lets players "rally" a species to improve Secret Ability odds (1/256 → 1/64 with a SA leader) and gleaming odds (down to 1/128 with charm + ×16 boost). — [Loomian Legacy Wiki: Rally Ranch](https://loomian-legacy.fandom.com/wiki/Rally_Ranch); [Loomian Legacy Wiki: Gleaming Loomian](https://loomian-legacy.fandom.com/wiki/Gleaming_Loomian)
- **Pokémon GO (exploration, event-boosted):** wild base 1/512; eggs/raids/research days use distinct boosted rates (1/10–1/25). — [Pokémon DB: GO shinies](https://pokemondb.net/go/shiny); [esports.gg: Research Day shiny odds](https://esports.gg/guides/pokemon/what-are-pokemon-go-research-day-shiny-odds/)
- **Steal a Brainrot (hybrid: visible spawn + steal):** brainrots spawn on a central conveyor; Secrets spawn rarely (<0.001%), announced server-wide, and can be stolen from other players' bases. Server Luck purchases and the 2-hourly Witching Hour modulate spawns. — [Steal a Brainrot Wiki: Secret](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Secret); [u4gm: how often Secrets spawn](https://www.u4gm.com/steal-a-brainrot/blog-how-often-do-secrets-spawn-in-steal-a-brainrot); [g4mmo: how to spawn brainrots](https://www.g4mmo.com/how-to-spawn-brainrots-in-steal-a-brainrot)
- **Pet Catchers (hybrid: world spawn + catch roll):** pets spawn in the world and are caught with cubes; "the better the cube rarity, the higher the chance," with Perfect Cubes at 100% success. Secrets spawn at 1/200,000–1/80,000,000. — [Pet Catchers Wiki: Items](https://petcatchers.fandom.com/wiki/Items); [Pet Catchers Wiki: Secret Pets](https://petcatchers.fandom.com/wiki/Secret_Pets); [Pro Game Guides: all Pet Catchers pets](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-pets-in-pet-catchers-and-how-to-catch-them-roblox/)
- **Grow a Garden (hatching + world interaction):** eggs hatch on timers, "plants grow in real time and have to be harvested and replanted," and "many rewards are randomized, such as seed boxes or pet eggs with uncertain outcomes and low-probability 'rare' items." — [Free Systems: The Algorithm Behind Steal a Brainrot](https://freesystems.substack.com/p/the-algorithm-behind-steal-a-brainrot)
- **Creatures of Sonaria (time-earned + survival):** Time Points accrue only while spawned-in (1/min), so creature acquisition is tied directly to session time in the survival world. — [CoS Wiki: Game Currencies](https://creatures-of-sonaria-official.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Currencies)
- **Neko Atsume (passive attraction):** cats are attracted by placing specific food and goodies; rare cats require particular items before they visit. — [Pocket Gamer: rare cats guide](https://www.pocketgamer.com/neko-atsume/rare-cats-guide/)
- Scale signal: Steal a Brainrot averaged ~1M CCU in September 2025. — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Commentary: "Grow a Garden shows how quickly an innocuous idea can turn into a global, highly optimized behavioral machine once you plug it into the right economic engine." — [Free Systems: The Algorithm Behind Steal a Brainrot](https://freesystems.substack.com/p/the-algorithm-behind-steal-a-brainrot)

### Inferences
- Exploration systems export rarity into the *world state* (location, weather, time, gear), which lets designers add content by adding places and conditions rather than new egg pools. Hatching systems export rarity into *volume*, which monetizes well (luck boosts, hatch speed, multi-hatch) but needs constant new eggs.
- The 2025–2026 Roblox leaders are hybrids in which the rare thing is physically present in the shared server (conveyor, world spawn) so that other players witness the find. This suggests "visible spawn + contest" is currently out-performing pure egg-hatching in attention, though I found no retention data to prove it.
- For a spaceship-building alien game, the Fisch/Palworld pattern (rarity gated by biome + tool + condition) maps more naturally onto "exploring planets" than egg hatching does, and the Steal a Brainrot pattern (server-announced spawn) is a cheap way to make discovery social.

- No quantitative retention/engagement comparison between exploration-based and hatch-based acquisition was found; the Naavik/Deconstructor of Fun analyses targeted in the brief did not surface in search, and no GDC talk on this comparison was located.
- Fisch's location/time/weather/season gating for specific fish and its bestiary were not retrieved.
- Palworld's Lucky (shiny-equivalent) and Alpha pal spawn odds and their audio/visual cues were not retrieved (search budget exhausted).

## Key Question 5: What is known about Palworld's "pals with work suitability" model and how could it map to "aliens that help build a spaceship"?

### Takeaway
Palworld assigns each pal a subset of 13 work types at a level 1–8 (max 10 after condensing/books); higher level = faster completion; pals auto-select tasks they are suited for; duplicates are condensed (48 copies for 4 stars) into +7 work speed per star and +1 suitability; passives add up to ~+175% work speed. The system is explicitly role-specialization-first, speed-second, which is the opposite emphasis from Roblox income simulators.

### Cited Findings
- "Work Suitability determines which jobs a Pal can perform and how efficiently it completes them. Palworld 1.0 expanded Work Suitability to a maximum of Level 10." Natural range is 1–8. "A higher level generally means the Pal completes suitable tasks faster." — [Nodecraft: work suitability level 10](https://nodecraft.com/support/games/palworld/general/palworld-work-suitability-level-10-explained); [Palworld Wiki: Working](https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Working)
- There are 13 work suitabilities, including Kindling, Watering, Planting, Handiwork, Mining and Transporting. — [Corrosion Hour: work suitability guide](https://www.corrosionhour.com/palworld-work-suitability-guide/); [Physgun: work suitability chart](https://physgun.com/tools/palworld-work-suitability/)
- Suitability can be raised +1 per Pal Essence Condenser use (four uses per pal): one suitability per use for stars 1–3 (best, second-best, third-best job) and all suitabilities at star 4; also via applied technique books, or temporarily via other pals' partner skills. — [Palmods: work suitability 1.0](https://www.palmods.gg/guides/whats-new/work-suitability); [Fextralife: Work Suitability](https://palworld.wiki.fextralife.com/Work_Suitability)
- Condensing costs 4/8/12/24 pals for stars 1–4 (48 total) and each star adds +7 Work Speed. — [palworldcalc: condensing calculator](https://palworldcalc.com/condensing-calculator/); [Palworld Wiki: Pal Essence Condenser](https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Pal_Essence_Condenser)
- "Work Speed is the stat that determines how fast a Pal completes each action cycle at a crafting station, with a Pal at Work Speed 70 finishing roughly twice as fast as one with Work Speed 35." Pathfinding is a real constraint: "keep station-to-station distance under 5 foundation tiles." — [Switchblade Gaming: base building guide](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/palworld/base-building-guide/); [Game8: Work Speed explained](https://game8.co/games/Palworld/archives/447263)
- Passive stack for workers: Artisan +50%, Work Slave +30% (−30% Attack), Serious +20%, Remarkable Craftsmanship; ~+175% total, stacking multiplicatively with food, building and Pal Soul buffs. — [Palworld Wiki: Artisan](https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Artisan); [Godlike: best passives](https://godlike.host/best-passive-skills-in-palworld-en-blog/)
- Sanity-draining negative passives should be avoided for base workers. — [Godlike: best passives](https://godlike.host/best-passive-skills-in-palworld-en-blog/)

### Inferences
- Mapping to "aliens that help build a spaceship": define ~6–10 spaceship work types (e.g., Welding ≈ Kindling, Wiring ≈ Electricity, Hauling ≈ Transporting, Hull Assembly ≈ Handiwork, Mining, Life Support ≈ Watering/Cooling, Navigation ≈ Medicine/intellect). Give each alien 1–3 suitabilities at level 1–4 (natural) with a condensing path to higher levels. Rarity then means *more suitabilities and higher starting levels*, not a flat income multiplier, so a Common "Welder lvl 2" stays useful even after a Legendary "Welder lvl 4 / Wiring lvl 3" appears, because two welders beat one and slot-limited roles are still filled by commons.
- Palworld's "pals walk between stations" constraint is the mechanic that makes *more* workers useful rather than only *better* workers; a spaceship build site with physically separated stations would reproduce this naturally.
- The 48-duplicate condensing cost is a strong duplicate sink that also generates its own loop ("catch 48 more of this common alien"); Roblox players already accept comparable sinks (10 Golden → 1 Rainbow in PS99, 16 copies → Mega Neon in Adopt Me).

### Gaps
- Exact per-level work speed values for suitability levels 1–10 (e.g., whether level 2 is 2x or 1.5x level 1) were not retrieved; sources only confirm "higher = faster" and the +7 per condensing star.
- Palworld pal rarity (Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary) versus suitability distribution was not retrieved.
- No developer interview or GDC talk on the design intent behind work suitability was found.

## Key Question 6: What role do collection indexes/encyclopedias and completion rewards play?

### Takeaway
Indexes convert "I didn't get the rare" into "I filled a slot," and the strongest versions pay out currency per new entry (PS99 Index Rewards), attach a long deterministic quest to the rarest entry (Pokémon GO Masterwork Research), or award a unique collectible for sustained interaction with each creature (Neko Atsume mementos). Evidence retrieved this session is thinner than for odds/multipliers.

### Cited Findings
- PS99 "Index Rewards allow you to earn rewards for collecting new pets," announced in the Heaven Update. — [BIG Games: Heaven Update](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-5)
- Pokémon GO's Masterwork Research "Sparkle and Shine" has 10 parts, does not expire once picked up, and culminates in a Shiny Diancie encounter; it was granted to GO Tour: Kalos ticket holders. — [Serebii: Sparkle and Shine](https://www.serebii.net/pokemongo/masterworkresearch/sparkleandshine.shtml); [GameRant: Sparkle and Shine steps](https://gamerant.com/pokemon-go-sparkle-shine-masterwork-research-steps-rewards-shiny-diancie/)
- Neko Atsume mementos are awarded only after several visits and only if the cat enjoyed the yard; each cat has a unique themed memento, making the memento set a second, slower collection layered on the cat collection. — [Twinfinite: all mementos](https://twinfinite.net/guides/neko-atsume-how-to-get-all-mementos/); [nekoatsumeguide: mementos](http://nekoatsumeguide.weebly.com/mementos.html)
- Adopt Me's collection is heavily retired (659 of 785 pets unobtainable), making "completion" a trading goal rather than a gameplay goal. — [BloxGuides: Adopt Me wiki](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/adopt-me)
- PS99's catalogue size (2,720 pets, 790 eggs) and Fisch's 151 mutations × many rarities indicate indexes are built to be effectively uncompletable, which keeps the index a permanent motivator. — [BloxGuides: PS99 wiki](https://bloxguidesgg.com/blog/pet-simulator-99-wiki-launch-2720-pets-790-eggs-may-2026); [Bloxodes: Fisch mutations](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/fisch/mutations)

### Inferences
- Paying out on *first acquisition* (Index Rewards) is the cheapest way to make a Common drop feel non-zero late in a player's life: the Common is worthless as a unit but worth something as an index entry.
- Deterministic long quests for the rarest entries (Masterwork Research) are the mobile-gacha answer to pity: the player who never rolls the shiny can still earn exactly one.
- A two-layer index (creature + memento/variant per creature) multiplies completion targets without adding creatures — directly reusable for aliens (alien + "artifact it brings back" or alien + mutation variant).

## Key Question 7: How do trading economies emerge and what design choices enable or prevent them?

### Takeaway
Trading economies emerge wherever (1) items are account-transferable, (2) supply is made finite by retirement/limited events, and (3) the community can observe scarcity. None of the games publishes official values; third-party value lists track closed trades. Design levers that shape the economy are variant tiers that act as value multipliers (Neon 2–4x, Mega 5–10x), permanently retired content (Adopt Me, "OG" rarity in Steal a Brainrot), and investment attributes that travel with the item (Creatures of Sonaria specs, PS99 enchants/levels).

### Cited Findings
- Adopt Me "has no official values and never shows a price in the trade window — every value list tracks what trades actually close at in trading servers and Discords." Demand is the most important factor. — [Beebom: Adopt Me values](https://beebom.com/roblox-adopt-me-pets-trading-value-list/); [Twinfinite: Adopt Me trade values](https://twinfinite.net/roblox/roblox-all-adopt-me-trade-values/)
- Neon pets carry a 2–4x value increase and Mega Neons 5–10x over the base pet; sample values: Bat Dragon 448 RP, Shadow Dragon 298 RP, Giraffe 202 RP, Frost Dragon 156 RP. Shadow and Frost Dragon are valuable because they are from 2019 events; Bat Dragon because of the candy grind required; "older items are more valuable than newer ones since their limited supply ramps up value over time." — [pnmmedia: Adopt Me values](https://www.pnmmedia.com/adopt-me-values/); [Driffle: Adopt Me trading values](https://driffle.com/blog/adopt-me-pet-trading-values/); [GamsGo: Adopt Me trading guide](https://www.gamsgo.com/blog/adopt-me-trading-values-guide)
- PS99 has a dedicated third-party value/RAP (recent average price) ecosystem. — [PS99 Wiki values/RAP](https://ps99wiki.com/); [game.guide: PS99 value list](https://www.game.guide/pet-simulator-99-value-list)
- Creatures of Sonaria trades in "mush": permanent-gacha creatures rarely exceed 500 mush except at release, limited-gacha and achievement creatures reach thousands, Somnia Elus ~500,000, Keruku 80,000–120,000+; a fully spec'd creature is worth 40–70% more. — [game.guide: CoS value list](https://www.game.guide/creatures-of-sonaria-value-list); [pinkcrow: CoS value list](https://pinkcrow.net/roblox/creatures-of-sonaria-value-list/)
- BGSI has community trade value lists for pets including Secret/Shiny/Mythic variants. — [Destructoid: BGSI trade value list](https://www.destructoid.com/bubble-gum-simulator-infinity-trade-value-list/); [Sportskeeda: BGSI value list](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/bubble-gum-simulator-infinity-value-list)
- Steal a Brainrot's "OG" rarity exists as a separate top tier and the rarest brainrots have tracked trading values. — [Steal a Brainrot Wiki: Rarities](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Rarities); [Eldorado: rarest brainrots trading value](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/steal-a-brainrot-rarest-brainrots-guide/)
- Grow a Garden has community trade-value calculators for mutated crops and pets. — [growagardentradevalues: mutation calculator](https://growagardentradevalues.com/calculator/mutation/)
- Real-money (grey-market) trading exists for PS99 Huges, BGSI Secret Shiny Mythics and GaG mutation pets. — [eBay: PS99 Huge pets](https://www.ebay.de/itm/204646010312); [eBay: BGSI pets](https://www.ebay.de/itm/405799030915); [eBay: GaG mutation pets](https://www.ebay.de/itm/388774750232)

### Inferences
- The economies are enabled by three decisions designers control: transferability, finite supply (retirement/limited), and legible variants. Remove any one and trading collapses into a market for only the newest content.
- Variant systems double as the economy's unit of account: Neon/Mega in Adopt Me and Shiny/Mythic in BGSI create standardized "grades" of the same creature, which is what lets community value lists exist at all.
- Investment attributes that travel with the creature (specs, levels, enchants) prevent a pure "tier = price" economy and reward play time, but also raise scam risk because value becomes harder to read in a trade window.
